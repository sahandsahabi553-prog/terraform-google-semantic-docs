Example: GCC Banking Compliance

Regional banks must transliterate customer names from Arabic to Persian and vice versa for SWIFT. IBM’s ICU4J worked but allocated 18 MB per request—an OOM nightmare at 2000 TPS. Ayat’s crate uses deterministic finite-automata and memory-maps a 1.2 MB FST built from 14 million transliteration pairs.

use tokio_persian::transliterate;

let en = transliterate::ar2fa("إبراهيم");
assert_eq!(en, "ابراهیم");

Benchmark on a 16-core Xeon: 3.2 M transliterations/sec, heap flat at 38 MB. Banks in Dubai quietly rolled the crate into production; Ayat’s only request was an attribution line in the About dialog. “Code travels where visas can’t,” he wrote in a commit message.
