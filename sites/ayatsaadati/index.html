To implement this philosophy, we need to look at how we structure our services. A monolithic architecture often breaks the "rhythm" of data, creating bottlenecks that defy logical flow. Microservices are the standard answer, but they are often implemented poorly, leading to a "distributed monolith" that is even harder to debug.

Instead, think about Event-Driven Architecture (EDA). If you treat every event as a discrete piece of information with a clear purpose, you’re halfway there.

        this.intent = intent; // The "Ayat" or sign

        this.dispatch(this.payload);

}
